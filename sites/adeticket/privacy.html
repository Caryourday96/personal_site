PRIVACY

What happens to the information you use here.

This notice covers Adeticket’s public portfolio and guides, Grouper and Bible Blitz. Other linked apps have separate accounts and data flows; check their notices before entering private records.

Updated October 22, 2026

Visiting the public pages

You can read the portfolio and guides without an account. Loading a page sends ordinary web request information, such as your IP address and browser information, to the hosting services that deliver it. Clicking a link to another service sends you to that service.

Grouper: participant lists stay in the browser

Grouper processes names and makes the draw in your browser. It does not upload the list or save it in application storage. Copy results writes the text to your clipboard when you choose it; Print opens your browser’s print flow. Copied or printed results may include the names of omitted participants.

Your browser may restore form text after reopening a tab. On a shared device, use Clear form and check the page before leaving it. Keep your own copy of a draw if you need it later.

Bible Blitz: local play and online rooms

Local Party and Solo games run in the browser. Solo personal bests are saved in that browser’s local storage. Clearing the site’s browser data removes those saved bests. Local games are not saved for recovery after a reload.

Hosted and online games send room codes, display names, team choices, answers and game actions to the realtime game service so connected devices can share the game. Player names, team information and scores can be visible to other participants. Keep display names suitable for sharing and do not enter sensitive information.

The browser stores room-session details, including the room code, role and connection credential, in session storage to reconnect the tab. These credentials grant access to that session; do not share them. Rooms are held in server memory for up to six hours, expire after one hour without room activity, and may end on a server restart. This describes game-room state, not a retention promise for hosting or security logs.

The host’s answer plan is restricted to the host view. Published presentation titles, announcements and winner messages are sent to connected displays. Keep private information out of those fields. Host TV controls load Google’s Cast software. Casting sends the receiver a room code and connection request identifier so it can join as a display.

Advertising and your choices

Advertising scripts on the portfolio homepage are currently disabled. The site retains AdSense ownership-verification information, which does not itself load advertising software. Earlier versions loaded Google’s advertising script; your browser may still contain cookies from those visits.

If advertising is enabled in the future, this notice will be updated to explain it. Google and other advertising vendors may use cookies to personalize ads based on visits to this and other sites. You can manage Google’s personalized advertising through Google My Ad Center and find choices for participating third-party advertisers at YourAdChoices.

Read how Google uses information from partner sites for its data practices. Browser settings can also control cookies and site storage. Those settings may affect saved preferences or sessions.

Contact and data questions

Email sent to adeticket@gmail.com is used to respond to your message. Include the app or page involved and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords, room-session credentials, financial records or health details.

Use the same address for privacy questions or a data request. Because Grouper does not upload participant lists and Solo bests are browser-local, manage those records on the device where you used them.
