01 / THE RULES

Two at a time.

Turn over two cards. A matching pair stays face up. Different cards turn back after a moment. Find all the pairs to finish.

02 / YOUR OWN PACE

A fresh little challenge.

Every board is shuffled. Try fewer moves, then a faster time. Use Tab or arrow keys to move and Enter or Space to flip. In two-player mode, matching earns a point and another turn; a miss passes play.

03 / JUST YOUR BROWSER
