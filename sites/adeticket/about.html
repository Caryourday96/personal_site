ABOUT ADETICKET

Small tools for the things people actually do.

Adeticket is the home for a growing set of independent web projects: Friends Showdown, Bible Blitz, Grouper, a private fitness tracker, a budget planner, the Fitdays workout-day view and Hydro Compare.

Why these projects sit together

Each app starts with a specific job. A game host needs to run a round without juggling paper answers. Someone logging exercise needs to see what they planned and what they actually completed. A budget works better when paydays and bills appear on the dates they occur. These are different products, but they share a preference for clear next steps and records you can review.

Public and private spaces

Friends Showdown, Bible Blitz, Grouper, Fitdays and Hydro Compare have public entry points. Fitness and budget records belong behind their own sign-in flows. The portfolio links to these services; it does not display your private entries. Check each app before sharing a screen or link, especially if you are using a public audience display.

Guides grounded in the tools

Our public guides explain the controls available in the apps, use clearly labeled examples and describe practical limits. Start with Bible Blitz rules and hosting, Grouper worked examples, or the game-night checklist. They are written to help you complete a task before and during an event.

If an instruction no longer matches what you see, email us with the page address and the step that needs checking. Do not include private fitness, financial or account records.

What to try next

Browse the project list, follow the app guide, or contact adeticket@gmail.com with a question or a reproducible issue. Feedback about confusing steps is useful while these tools are being refined.
